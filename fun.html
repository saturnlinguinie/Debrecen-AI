Outdoor & Nature

Debrecen's Great Forest (Nagyerdő) is the green heart of the city.

🦁 Zoo & Amusement Park

Nagyerdei Kultúrpark

Hungary's first rural zoo, home to 900 animals from 170 species across 17 hectares. Includes the country's only permanent amusement park with bumper cars, a vintage carousel, and a small train.

Address: 4032 Debrecen, Ady Endre út 1. Tickets from ~3,900 Ft (child) / 4,900 Ft (adult) [citation:8][citation:13].
♨️ Thermal Spa

Aquaticum Debrecen

Thermal baths, a Mediterranean indoor aqua park with slides, a sauna world, and wellness treatments. Located right in the Nagyerdő forest.

Address: Nagyerdei park 1. Indoor pools are open year-round [citation:3].
🌳 Great Forest

Nagyerdő Park

Walking trails, Lake Békás with fountains, picnic spots, and an open-air stage with summer concerts and cinema. Ranked by Tripadvisor as one of Hungary's best attractions.

Free to explore. The university main building is also here [citation:3].

Indoor Activities

Rainy day? These are perfect for groups and student budgets.

🔐 Escape Room

Exit the Room

Immersive escape rooms with themed stories (prison break, jungle adventure, mystery investigations). For groups of 2–6 people. Two central locations.

Address: Péterfia u. 39 & Miklós u. 34. Website: exittheroom.hu [citation:5].
🔐 Escape Room

Game of Rooms

Escape rooms with cinematic themes including detective mysteries and magician chambers. 60-minute challenges for 2–6 players.

Address: Szent Anna u. 31. Phone: +36 30 145 8783 [citation:10].
🎳 Bowling & Bar

Campus Bowling Bar

Bowling lanes, darts, billiards, and a full bar. Located right at the Campus Hotel — perfect for a student night out.

Address: Kassai út 26. Website: campusbowling.hu [citation:11].
🤸 Trampoline Park

Debrecen Trampoline Park

Eastern Hungary's first trampoline park with 45 trampolines, an arcade, bouncy castle, and base jump attraction. Designed for university students too.

1,600 square meters. Check local listings for current opening hours [citation:1].

Festivals & Events

Debrecen has events year-round. Here are the biggest ones.

🍷 Summer

Debrecen Wine & Jazz Days

Wine tasting and live jazz by Lake Békás. Over 40 music acts and wines from across the Carpathian Basin.

Usually early August. Check visitdebrecen.com for dates [citation:12].
🌸 August

Flower Carnival (Virágkarnevál)

Debrecen's most famous event. A massive parade with flower-covered floats and dancers. The climax is on August 20th.

One of Hungary's biggest festivals. Hundreds of thousands attend [citation:12].
🎵 June

Campus Festival

Hungary's biggest student festival, held right in Debrecen. Concerts, parties, and events for university students.

Usually in June. Check campusfesztival.hu for lineup and tickets [citation:2].

💡 Student Tip: Many places offer student discounts. Always ask! The Aquaticum and escape rooms often have reduced prices with a valid student ID.
