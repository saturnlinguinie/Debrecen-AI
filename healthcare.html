Get Your TAJ Card

TAJ Card (Health Insurance)

Stipendium Hungaricum students are entitled to a free TAJ card. Apply as soon as possible.

📄 Documents Needed

What to Bring

Passport, residence permit, address card, Letter of Award, and student status certificate.

You can start the application during your first personal registration.
🏢 Where to Apply

National Health Insurance Fund

4026 Debrecen, Darabos Street 11

Applying in person can be faster than waiting for the card by post.
⏱️ Processing Time

Be Patient

The card can take several weeks to arrive. Keep your paperwork safe and follow up if needed.

Without a TAJ number, you may be charged for medical care.
