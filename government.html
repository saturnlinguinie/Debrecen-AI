Address Card (Lakcímkártya)

Your address card proves where you live. You need it for bank accounts, phone contracts, and the TAJ card.

📄 What It Is

Proof of Address

A document with your name, address, and a QR code. Keep it safe — you'll need it often.

If you move, you must report the change to the Immigration Authority.
🏢 How to Get It

At the Immigration Office

If you didn't receive it by email or at the embassy, go to the immigration office. Ask at reception — you may get it without waiting for a number.

Bring your passport and residence permit.
