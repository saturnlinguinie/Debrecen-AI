University Dormitories

The University of Debrecen offers limited dorm rooms. Prices below are per semester for the 5-month contract, paid in one installment upon arrival [citation:1].

🏫 Böszörményi Campus

Veres Péter Dormitory

200,000 HUF/semester (approx. 40,000 HUF/month). 3-bed rooms next to the Faculty of Economics and Agricultural Sciences.

Cheapest option. Shared rooms. Canteen on ground floor .
🏫 Main Campus

Kossuth Lajos Dormitory III

44,150 HUF/month. 2×3-bed rooms next to the Main Building. Recommended for Humanities, Science, Medicine, Pharmacy, and Dentistry students.

Internet and TV in every room. Library with English books .
🏫 Kassai Campus

Campus Hotel

278,250 HUF/semester. Double rooms with private bathroom. 100 meters from the Faculty of Public Health, Law, and Informatics.

Shared kitchens available. More expensive but more private [citation:1].
🏥 Clinic Area

Markusovszky Lajos Dormitory III

235,750 HUF/semester (approx. 45,150 HUF/month). 3-bed rooms near the university clinics.

Recommended for Medicine, Pharmacy, and Dentistry students [citation:1].

All dorm contracts are for 5 months and must be paid in full upon arrival. There is an additional 9,000 HUF maintenance and cultural contribution fee [citation:1].

Dormitory Application Timeline (2026/2027)

First-year Stipendium Hungaricum students must apply online during this short window .

📅 Application Opens

6 July 2026, 10:00 AM

The online application form becomes available on the Dormitory Directorate website.

Apply through kollegiumok.unideb.hu .
⏰ Deadline

8 July 2026, 11:59 PM

Applications submitted after this deadline cannot be accepted.

Only a 2-day window. Set a reminder .
📧 Results

14 July 2026

Applicants are informed about the result by email.

If admitted, you must return the signed Accommodation Conditions document by 2 August 2026 .
🔑 Move-In

From 1 September 2026

Admitted students can move in from this date.

A 15-day grace period applies only to students who move in by 15 September .

Typical rental prices (university guide): Room: below 160,000 HUF · Studio: 160,000–200,000 HUF · 1-bedroom: 200,000–300,000 HUF · 2-bedroom: 300,000+ HUF [citation:10].

Always visit a flat in person before paying a deposit. The security deposit is typically 2 months' rent. Read the contract carefully before signing [citation:10].

⚠️ Never send money before viewing a flat in person. Scammers target international students.
