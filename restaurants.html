Hungarian Food

Try these local dishes at least once during your stay.

🍲 Traditional

Goulash (Gulyás)

Hungary's most famous dish. A hearty soup with beef, potatoes, and paprika.

Look for "Gulyásleves" on menus.
🥘 Traditional

Lángos

Deep-fried flatbread topped with sour cream and cheese. A cheap street food classic.

Find it at markets and food stalls.
🍰 Sweet

Chimney Cake (Kürtőskalács)

Sweet dough rolled in sugar and baked on a spit. Popular at festivals.

Best eaten warm.

Student-Friendly Areas

Where to find affordable food near campus and the city center.

📍 City Center

Good for lunch between classes.
📍 Near Campus

Nagyerdei Area

Cafes and restaurants near the university campus and the Great Forest (Nagyerdő).

Popular with students between lectures.
🛒 Budget Tip

Supermarkets

For the cheapest option, cook at home. Aldi, Lidl, Tesco, and SPAR are all in Debrecen.

A weekly grocery shop is far cheaper than eating out.
