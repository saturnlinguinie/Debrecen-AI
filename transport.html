Local Transport (DKV)

DKV operates the buses, trolleybuses, and trams in Debrecen.

🎫 Student Pass

Monthly Student Pass

5,300 Ft (electronic) or 5,500 Ft (paper). Valid on all DKV bus, trolleybus, and tram services.

Buy online or at DKV ticket offices. You'll need a student ID.
📱 Mobile Tickets

Buy Before You Board

Single tickets are cheaper on the app than buying from the driver. Download the Nemzeti Mobilfizetési app.

On-board tickets cost significantly more.

Airport Transfer

Debrecen Airport is close to the city. Here's how to get in.

🚌 Public Bus

Airport Bus

Bus Airport 1 and Airport 2 connect the airport to the Railway Station (Nagyállomás) and the Main Campus area (Doberdó utca).

Takes about 12 minutes. Buy your ticket on the app before boarding.
🚕 Taxi

Taxi from Airport

Official taxis are available outside the terminal. More expensive than the bus but convenient with luggage.
